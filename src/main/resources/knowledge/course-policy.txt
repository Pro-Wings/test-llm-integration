COURSE DETAILS
The sample Java Backend Development course lasts six months.
It covers Core Java, Spring Boot, REST APIs, SQL, and introductory microservices.
---
CLASS SCHEDULE
Classes take place Monday through Saturday.
The class timing is 9:30 AM to 11:00 AM.
Sunday is a weekly holiday.
---
CANCELLATION POLICY
Students may cancel their enrollment within seven calendar days of enrollment.
A processing fee of INR 500 is deducted from the refund.
After seven calendar days, the course fee is non-refundable.
---
RECORDINGS
Students receive access to class recordings.
Recording access remains available for ninety days after course completion.
---
CERTIFICATE POLICY
Students need at least eighty percent attendance and must complete the final project
to receive a course completion certificate.
---
PAYMENT POLICY
The sample course fee is INR 30000.
Students may pay in three equal monthly installments of INR 10000.
---
Course Syllabus inculdes topics: Java Basics, Object-Oriented Programming, Exception Handling, Collections Framework, Java Streams, JDBC, Spring Boot Fundamentals, RESTful Web Services, SQL Queries, Database Design, and Introduction to Microservices Architecture.
