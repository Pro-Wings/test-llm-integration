CHARGING ACCESS POLICY
A driver may start charging only when the station has an available connector
and the vehicle is compatible with that connector.
An available connector does not guarantee vehicle compatibility.
Current connector availability must be obtained from the station status service.
---
CHARGING TARIFF POLICY
The charging tariff is the station's current price per kWh.
The applicable station tariff must be obtained from the station status service.
This policy document does not contain station-specific charging prices.
---
IDLE PARKING POLICY
After charging completes, the driver has a ten-minute grace period
to disconnect the vehicle and vacate the charging bay.
After the grace period, an idle parking fee of INR 2 per minute applies.
The idle parking fee is separate from the charging energy cost.
---
UNAVAILABLE CONNECTOR POLICY
If a station has zero available connectors, the driver cannot start charging
immediately at that station.
The driver should wait for a connector to become available or choose another station.
This document does not provide waiting-time estimates.
---
CHARGING SESSION CANCELLATION POLICY
A driver may cancel before energy delivery begins without a cancellation fee.
After energy delivery begins, the driver must pay for the energy already delivered.
---
PAYMENT POLICY
Supported payment methods are UPI and wallet.
A payment receipt is generated after the charging session is settled.
---
STATION SUPPORT POLICY
If a station is not found, the driver should verify the station ID.
A missing station record must not be interpreted as zero available connectors.
Vehicle compatibility and reservation availability are not provided by this policy document.
